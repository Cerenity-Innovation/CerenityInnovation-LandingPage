What it does

TIDEYA integrates multimodal data — from real-time wearable vitals to daily schedules and environmental factors — to align your mind and body, turning complex data into affirming, actionable insights.

Energy forecasting

Visualise your daily capacity.

We predict your daily energy capacity by syncing your real-time vitals with your personal baseline — gently guiding your day so you can pace yourself based on the energy you actually have available.

Stress patterns

Decode the hidden patterns of stress.

Our AI looks beyond the obvious, surfacing how external factors — a sudden shift in weather, a high density of meetings — weigh on your unique limits and add to your stress.

Personalised insights

From data to affirmation.

We transform complex, multimodal data into timely, resonating, affirmative messages — delivered exactly when you need them, as gentle reminders that keep you grounded and aligned with yourself.

Google Calendar

Your schedule, read-only.

If you connect Google Calendar, TIDEYA reads the calendars you select to estimate your daily schedule load, shape your energy forecast, and generate your personalised insights.

TIDEYA requests read-only access. It cannot create, change, or delete your events or calendars.

What TIDEYA reads and stores

  • Your calendar list, including each calendar's ID, name, colour, your access level, and whether it is your primary calendar, so that you can choose which calendars TIDEYA reads. We store only the IDs of the calendars you select.
  • Event timing: start time, end time, duration, and whether the event is all-day.
  • Event status: whether the event is cancelled, shows you as busy or free, or is recurring, and its type in Google Calendar, such as focus time or out of office.
  • Attendance: the number of attendees, a summary of whether other attendees share your email domain, your response status, whether you are the organiser, and whether your attendance is optional.
  • Links and identifiers: whether the event has a video link, and identifiers for the event, its recurring series, and the calendar it came from.
  • Event titles, the first 500 characters of event descriptions, and event locations, stored only if you turn on the "Use event titles" setting.

Google returns the full record of each event to TIDEYA, which can include titles, descriptions, and attendee names and email addresses. We use attendee email addresses only to produce the domain summary above, and we do not store attendee names or email addresses.

We also store the access and refresh tokens Google issues so that TIDEYA can sync your calendar.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our Privacy Policy explains how this information is used, stored, shared, and deleted.

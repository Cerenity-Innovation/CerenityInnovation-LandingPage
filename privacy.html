1. Introduction

At Cerenity, we believe that meaningful technology should respect the people it serves.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cerenity Innovation, Inc. ("Cerenity," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, stores, shares, and protects personal information in connection with the TIDEYA app (also referred to in our materials as the "Cerenity App") and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Our Services are designed to help users better understand their daily energy levels, stress-related patterns, and personal well-being through AI-powered analysis of physiological and contextual information.

Artificial intelligence is a core part of Cerenity. In addition to using personal information to generate individualised wellness insights, Cerenity may, with appropriate notice and consent, use eligible personal wellness information to develop, train, test, evaluate, and improve shared Cerenity AI models used across the Services. Google Calendar data and other Google user data are excluded from shared AI model training, as described in Sections 5.8 and 13.

Because some of the information processed through Cerenity may be sensitive, we are committed to providing transparency, meaningful user control, appropriate consent mechanisms, and safeguards proportionate to the nature of the information.

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed by Cerenity. Connected third-party platforms may have separate privacy policies governing their independent processing activities.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you enable and the permissions you grant, we may collect and process the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account identifiers.
  • Authentication information.
  • Account preferences and settings.

We use this information to establish and manage your account, provide support, maintain security, and communicate with you.

2.2 Physiological and Wellness Information

With your authorisation, we may access and process physiological and wellness information from compatible devices and connected services.

Depending on the integrations you enable, this may include:

  • Heart rate.
  • Heart rate variability.
  • Sleep duration and sleep patterns.
  • Activity and movement information.
  • Exercise-related information.
  • Physiological trends and measurements.
  • Other wellness measurements supported by connected devices and specifically disclosed when access is requested.

We use such information to establish personal baselines, estimate energy capacity, identify patterns and changes over time, generate personalised wellness insights, and, where permitted and consented to, improve Cerenity's shared AI models.

Some of this information may constitute sensitive personal information or receive heightened protection under applicable law.

2.3 Calendar and Schedule Information

With your permission, we access read-only information from the Google Calendars you select to analyse how daily activities and scheduling patterns may relate to your energy and stress.

This may include:

  • Event dates and times.
  • Event duration.
  • Frequency of scheduled activities.
  • Schedule density and availability patterns.
  • Structural details of events, such as attendee counts, recurrence, and whether an event has a video link.
  • Event titles, descriptions, and locations, only if you turn on the "Use event titles" setting.

We will seek to access only calendar information reasonably necessary for the relevant Cerenity functionality.

Section 13 lists the specific Google Calendar information we access and explains how we use, store, share, and delete it.

2.4 Location and Environmental Information

Where you authorise location access or provide location information manually, we may process location information to obtain relevant environmental context.

This may be used to identify factors such as:

  • Weather conditions.
  • Temperature.
  • Environmental changes.
  • Other location-dependent conditions relevant to your wellness insights.

The precision and frequency of location processing will depend on the permissions you provide and the features you use.

2.5 User-Provided Information

We may process information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Wellness preferences.
  • Daily check-ins.
  • Self-reported energy levels.
  • Self-reported stress levels.

This may include:

2.7 AI-Generated and Inferred Information

Cerenity's systems generate or infer information from the data described above.

This may include:

4. AI Processing and Personalization

AI-powered analysis is a core component of Cerenity.

Cerenity analyses authorised physiological, behavioural, scheduling, environmental, and user-provided information to identify patterns and generate personalised wellness insights.

Processing may occur:

  • On your device.
  • On Cerenity-controlled systems.
  • Through cloud infrastructure acting on behalf of Cerenity.
  • Through authorised AI or technology providers where permitted.

AI processing may generate new inferred information concerning your wellness, energy, stress-related patterns, behaviour, or activities.

These inferences may be inaccurate and should not be treated as medical conclusions.

Where personal information is transmitted to an external AI provider, Cerenity will clearly disclose the relevant sharing and obtain explicit permission where required by applicable law or platform requirements.

Google Calendar data and other Google user data, including information derived from them, are never provided to a third-party AI provider to train or improve that provider's models or any other generalised AI model. Section 13.4 describes the limited summaries sent to OpenAI, with your permission, to generate your own insights.

5. Shared AI Model Training and Improvement

5.1 Why We Train Shared Models

Cerenity develops shared AI models that learn from patterns across data contributed by multiple users.

The purpose of shared model training is to improve the Cerenity Services, including:

  • Improving the accuracy of energy forecasts.
  • Improving the identification of wellness-related patterns.
  • Improving personalised recommendations.
  • Identifying relationships among physiological, behavioural, and environmental factors.
  • Reducing incorrect or misleading outputs.
  • Evaluating model performance across different usage patterns.
  • Improving reliability, safety, and robustness.
  • Developing new Cerenity health and wellness functionality.

Shared models may subsequently be used to provide AI-powered functionality to multiple Cerenity users.

5.2 Information That May Be Used

Subject to your permissions, applicable law, platform requirements, and any required consent, information used for model improvement may include:

  • Physiological and wellness information.
  • Sleep and activity information.
  • Self-reported wellness information.
  • Energy and stress check-ins.
  • Environmental context.
  • Interaction and usage information.
  • AI-generated predictions and insights, except those produced using Google user data.
  • Feedback concerning the accuracy or usefulness of Cerenity outputs.
  • Other information reasonably necessary to evaluate or improve Cerenity's wellness models.

Information used for model improvement does not include Google Calendar data, other Google user data, or anything derived from them, as described in Section 5.8.

Cerenity seeks to minimise the use of direct identifiers such as names and email addresses in AI model-training datasets and to separate or reduce identifying information where reasonably practicable.

5.3 Consent

Where required by applicable law, Apple requirements, or other platform requirements, Cerenity will obtain separate affirmative consent before using eligible personal wellness information for shared AI model training or improvement.

The consent request will describe the nature and purpose of the processing.

Acceptance of Cerenity's Terms of Use alone does not replace any separate model-training consent that is required.

5.4 HealthKit and Similar Health Platform Information

Information obtained through Apple HealthKit is subject to additional restrictions.

Cerenity will only use HealthKit-derived information for purposes permitted under Apple's requirements and applicable law.

Where HealthKit-derived information contributes to model development, such processing will be limited to developing or improving the health and wellness functionality provided through Cerenity and will occur only with the permissions required for that processing.

Cerenity does not use HealthKit information for:

  • Advertising.
  • Marketing.
  • Sale to data brokers.
  • Unrelated profiling.
  • General-purpose data mining unrelated to the Cerenity health and wellness service.
  • Unrelated general-purpose AI training.

Cerenity will not provide HealthKit-derived personal information to a third-party AI provider for that provider's own independent or general-purpose model training.

5.5 Reducing Identifiability

Where reasonably practicable, Cerenity applies measures designed to reduce direct identification of users in datasets used for shared model improvement.

Depending on the processing involved, such measures may include separating direct account identifiers from training records, pseudonymisation, aggregation, access restrictions, or other privacy and security safeguards.

De-identification does not eliminate every possible privacy risk, and Cerenity therefore continues to apply safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information.

5.6 Withdrawal of Model-Training Consent

Where model-training processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent for future processing.

You may do so using privacy controls made available through Cerenity or by contacting:

info@cerenityinnovation.com

Following withdrawal, we will stop adding newly collected eligible personal information to shared AI model training based on that consent within a reasonable operational period.

Where identifiable source information remains separately stored and is subject to a valid deletion request, we will delete it in accordance with applicable law and our retention obligations.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing completed before withdrawal.

5.7 Information Already Incorporated Into AI Models

AI model training changes mathematical parameters within a model based on patterns found across many training examples.

As a result, once information has been lawfully incorporated into a shared model, it may not always be technically possible to identify or isolate the effect of a specific individual's contribution.

Withdrawal of consent therefore generally prevents future use of eligible personal data for new training but may not automatically reverse model training that has already occurred.

Where technically feasible and legally required, Cerenity will take additional measures to address applicable deletion or objection rights.

This provision does not limit any rights provided to you by applicable law.

5.8 Google Calendar and Other Google User Data

Information received through Google APIs, including Google Calendar data ("Google user data"), is subject to additional restrictions. These restrictions also cover anything aggregated, anonymised, or derived from Google user data, including calendar-influenced outputs such as predicted energy.

Cerenity uses Google user data to train or tune only your own personalised model, for the features TIDEYA provides to you. That model is not used for any other user.

Cerenity does not use Google user data to:

  • Develop, train, tune, or improve shared Cerenity AI models or any other generalised AI or machine-learning model.
  • Provide labels or features for a shared model.
  • Learn global model weights by pooling data across users.
  • Train or improve a third party's AI models.
  • Serve advertising, including targeted, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

Cerenity does not sell Google user data to anyone, including advertising platforms, data brokers, and information resellers.

These restrictions apply whether or not you have given consent to shared AI model training under Section 5.3.

13. Google User Data

This section explains how TIDEYA accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information received from Google APIs when you connect Google Calendar. It applies in addition to the rest of this Privacy Policy and controls if the two differ.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google API Services User Data Policy: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy
Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy: https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy

13.1 Information We Access

TIDEYA requests read-only access to Google Calendar. It cannot create, change, or delete your events or calendars.

So that you can choose which calendars TIDEYA reads, it reads your calendar list, including each calendar's ID, name, colour, your access level, and whether it is your primary calendar. We store only the IDs of the calendars you select.

For events on the calendars you select, we store:

  • Start time, end time, duration, and whether the event is all-day.
  • Whether the event is cancelled, shows you as busy or free, or is recurring, and its type in Google Calendar, such as focus time or out of office.
  • The number of attendees, and a summary of whether other attendees share your email domain.
  • Your response status, whether you are the organiser, and whether your attendance is optional.
  • Whether the event has a video link.
  • Identifiers for the event, its recurring series, and the calendar it came from.

We store event titles, the first 500 characters of event descriptions, and event locations only if you turn on the "Use event titles" setting.

Google returns the full record of each event to TIDEYA, which can include titles, descriptions, and attendee names and email addresses. We use attendee email addresses only to produce the domain summary above, and we do not store attendee names or email addresses.

We also store the access and refresh tokens Google issues so that TIDEYA can sync your calendar.

13.2 How We Use It

We use Google Calendar information only to provide and improve user-facing features of TIDEYA:

  • Estimating your daily schedule load and how your day is structured.
  • Shaping your energy forecast and showing how your schedule contributes to it.
  • Fitting and updating your own personal baselines and personalised model.
  • Generating your personalised insights and daily advice.

We also use it where necessary for security, including investigating abuse, and to comply with applicable law.

We may review aggregated, anonymised statistics for internal operations and product decisions. We do not use those statistics to train AI models.

13.3 How We Store and Protect It

Google Calendar information and tokens are stored in Cerenity's application database, on cloud infrastructure provided by Amazon Web Services, and are linked to your account. They are transmitted over encrypted connections, and access is limited to the systems and personnel that need it.

We do not allow people to read Google user data unless:

  • You have given explicit consent, for example to resolve a support request.
  • It is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating a bug or abuse.
  • It is necessary to comply with applicable law.
  • The data has been aggregated and anonymised and is used for internal operations.

13.4 How We Share It

We do not sell Google user data or share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

We transfer Google user data only:

  • To Amazon Web Services, which hosts our systems.
  • To OpenAI, with your permission, as described below.
  • Where necessary for security purposes or to comply with applicable law.
  • In a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with your explicit prior consent.

If you allow AI processing, TIDEYA sends OpenAI a short summary so that it can write your daily advice and chat replies. That summary can include the number of events on your calendar that day and general energy levels influenced by your calendar, such as how your energy is likely to arrive through the day. It does not include event titles, descriptions, locations, attendee details, or event times.

OpenAI is not permitted to use this information to train its models. If you do not allow AI processing, no information derived from Google user data is sent to OpenAI.

13.5 Retention and Deletion

We keep Google Calendar information while your account is active.

Disconnecting Google Calendar in TIDEYA asks Google to revoke TIDEYA's access, deletes our stored tokens and calendar selection, and stops new collection. Calendar information already synced stays in your account, and can continue to inform your history and personal baselines, until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.

You can also remove TIDEYA's access at any time from your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Turning off the "Use event titles" setting deletes the event titles, descriptions, and locations we have stored.

To delete your synced calendar information without deleting your account, contact info@cerenityinnovation.com.

Deleting your account asks Google to revoke TIDEYA's access and deletes the Google Calendar information associated with your account. Copies in routine backups may persist for a limited period until they expire.
